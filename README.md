MedGrama – AI-Powered Health Screening

Overview:-

MedGrama is an AI-assisted preliminary health screening solution designed to support healthcare workers at Primary Health Centres (PHCs).

Problem Statement:-

Early identification of common health conditions can be challenging in resource-limited healthcare settings.

Proposed Solution:-

- Collect symptoms and basic health information.
- Support preliminary identification of potential health risks.
- Help PHC workers prioritize patients for further evaluation.
- Encourage timely medical referrals.
